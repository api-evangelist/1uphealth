It’s easy to assume any vendor’s FHIR endpoint can satisfy this year’s mandate; and for a demo, that’s often true. But this is table stakes, not a differentiator. The question that actually separates vendors (and the one most buyers don’t think to ask until they’re locked into a contract) is what’s underneath that endpoint.
